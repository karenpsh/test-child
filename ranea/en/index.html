From Fujian to Penang

The Ghee Hiang story in Penang

The Ghee Hiang range is an important part of Penang's heritage.

  1. 1856

    Ghee Hiang is founded at the height of the tin and rubber boom in Malaya, as large numbers of immigrants arrive from China.

  2. Early 1900s

    Ghee Hiang Baby Brand Pure Sesame Oil is introduced, made by the traditional Fujian method that preserves its high quality and rich flavour.

  3. Today

    Ghee Hiang Baby Brand Sesame Oil is widely trusted and used in homes, the food industry, restaurants and hotels.

  4. The modern era

    With strong demand for healthier, more premium cooking, RANÉA Sesame Oil is launched to meet the needs of today's households.

The benefits of RANÉA sesame oil

A summary of the benefits of sesame oil supported by recent scientific studies:

  1. Rich in antioxidants: protect your cells

    Sesame oil contains lignans such as sesamin and sesamol, as well as vitamin E, which help fight free radicals and reduce inflammation.

  2. Heart health: lower bad cholesterol

    Studies show that regular intake of sesame oil can lower LDL cholesterol and triglycerides and raise HDL cholesterol, supporting heart health.

  3. Blood sugar control: steadier glucose levels

    Sesame intake has been linked to lower fasting blood glucose and HbA1c, showing potential in the management of type 2 diabetes.

  4. Bone health: help prevent osteoporosis

    Sesamin in sesame oil has been shown to improve bone mineral density and reduce the risk of osteoporosis, especially in postmenopausal women.

  5. Mental wellbeing: less stress and anxiety

    The aroma of sesame oil has been found to reduce the expression of stress-related genes, showing potential to ease anxiety and low mood.

  6. Skin protection: resist UV rays

    The antioxidants in sesame oil help protect skin from UV damage, making it a natural ingredient in sun-care products.

  7. Hair care: strengthen and protect the scalp

    Sesame oil has antibacterial and antifungal properties that help reduce dandruff, protect the scalp and improve overall hair health.

  8. These benefits show the potential of sesame oil as a valuable addition to a daily diet in support of overall health. It is important to consult a health professional before making major changes to your diet or health regimen.

Cooking tips

Stir-fries

Sesame oil in a stir-fry wakes the appetite with its warm, nutty aroma.

How to use it

  • Add a few drops at the end of cooking to lift the aroma and flavour of the dish.
  • Sprinkle over a stir-fry just before serving for a more refined taste.

Grilled food

Sesame oil on grilled food adds a sheen to the surface and deepens the flavour noticeably.

How to use it

  • Brush it on fish or other food to keep it juicier on the grill.

Marinades, sauces & dressings

Combine sesame oil with soy sauce, rice vinegar, honey and garlic for an Asian-style sauce or marinade.
